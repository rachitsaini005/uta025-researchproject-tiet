# Rights, attribution and publication checklist

This is collaborative academic work by the six team members listed in the README.

Before setting the GitHub repository to **Public**:
- Obtain agreement from all contributors to publish the report, poster, canvases and anonymised survey data.
- Confirm that the course/institute permits public redistribution of the submitted materials.
- Check that the poster and report do not contain third-party images, logos or material that cannot be redistributed.
- Review the anonymised CSVs for accidental identifiers or free-text disclosures before publication.

No open-source licence is included. Do not assume that public availability grants permission to reuse the team's materials.
