# Customer-validation results (aggregate only)

BioBlend's customer-validation work used two separate surveys:

- **B2B industrial ethanol survey:** 52 reported responses.
- **D2C household-products survey:** 183 reported responses.
- **Total:** 235 reported responses.

This repository intentionally does **not** publish respondent-level records, raw response exports, names, contact details, company identifiers, locations, timestamps, or free-text submissions. Only aggregate findings reported in the submitted project report are retained here.

## B2B industrial ethanol survey

| Reported finding | Aggregate result |
|---|---:|
| Respondents selecting purity/grade specifications as an important supplier factor | 84.6% |
| Respondents selecting price per litre | 69.2% |
| Respondents selecting supply reliability and consistency | 57.7% |
| Respondents rating sustainability as very or somewhat important | 80.8% |
| Respondents reporting familiarity with 2G ethanol (very or somewhat familiar) | 100% |

### Interpretation recorded in the project report

The report concludes that industrial buyers place strong emphasis on purity/grade specifications and price. It proposes a direct-sales-first approach for large industrial customers, with distributors as a secondary channel.

## D2C household-products survey

| Reported finding | Aggregate result |
|---|---:|
| Respondents preferring natural soil-enrichment options | 72.7% |
| Respondents very or somewhat interested in organic soil conditioner | 88% |
| Respondents interested in a plant-based adhesive | 94.6% |
| Respondents very or somewhat interested in a home-use fire extinguisher | 95% |
| Respondents willing to pay up to ₹800 for a small home fire extinguisher | 93.3% |

### Interpretation recorded in the project report

The report identifies natural soil conditioner as a promising household product, highlights bonding strength and safety as key adhesive purchase considerations, and identifies certification, availability and price as important for the proposed home fire extinguisher.

## Publication and privacy note

Percentages above are transcribed from the project's submitted report and retain its original reported denominators/rounding. They are aggregate summaries, not independently re-analysed estimates. No individual-level survey data is distributed with this repository.
